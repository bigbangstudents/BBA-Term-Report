Project Alpha Report Card SOP

1. Report Types:
• Mid-Term Report (Weeks 8–11): Used for Parent Consultation Day.
• Final Term Report (Post-Showcase): Submitted within 1 week after Showcase.

2. Balanced Grading Rules:
• A+ (Min 1, Max 5): Celebrates the student's standout strengths.
• A (Unlimited): Solid benchmark mastery.
• B (Min 1, Max 3): Essential growth area so parents see clear next steps for continuing next term.

3. Approval Flow:
Teacher Generates PDF ➔ Centre PIC Double Checks ➔ Send to Ops & Share with Parents
